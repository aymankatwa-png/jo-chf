For doctors and research staff

Follow these steps in order the first time. After that, use them as a reference. Every screen in Jo-CHF works the same way on a phone at the bedside and on a computer in the clinic.

1

Request your account

  1. Open Jo-CHF and click Request an account under the sign-in button.
  2. Write your full name, your username (your email, or a numeric ID given by the study team), your phone, your center, your role and your professional ID.
  3. Choose a password: at least 8 characters with letters and numbers.
  4. Press Send request. An administrator approves your account and confirms your center. Then you can sign in.
2

Sign in

Type your username and password and press Sign in.

  • For patient privacy, Jo-CHF signs you out after 30 minutes without activity. A thin red line at the top of the screen grows before that happens, and a message warns you 2 minutes before.
  • After 5 wrong passwords the account is locked for 15 minutes.
  • Forgot your password? Click Forgot password. An administrator can also give you a temporary password; you then choose a new one.
3

The dashboard

The first screen shows the whole registry. The big number is the total enrolled against the target of 1,000 patients. The red rhythm strip shows enrollment: each heartbeat is one month, and a taller beat means more patients enrolled that month.

Below, there is one card per center with recruitment against target, follow-up completion at 30 days, 6 months and 12 months, data completeness and overdue visits. Your center has a red ring and a dark border. You can open only your own center; other centers show summary numbers.

4

Your center page

Click your center card, or My center in the menu. Here you see:

  • Follow-ups due: patients whose visit window is open, opens within 7 days, or was missed.
  • Data queries from the coordinating team.
  • Patients: search by Study ID, name or file number. Click a column title to sort. Click a row to open the patient.
5

Add a new patient

  1. Press Add new patient.
  2. Enter the dates. Write dates as DD/MM/YYYY (the slashes appear by themselves) or use the calendar button.
  3. The consent date is required. Enrollment is blocked without signed consent.
  4. Tick all three inclusion criteria. If any exclusion criterion is ticked, the system stops enrollment.
  5. Choose inpatient or outpatient.
  6. Enter the patient's identifiers: name, phones, hospital file number and (optional) national ID. These are encrypted and only your center can see them.
  7. Press Enroll patient. The Study ID is created automatically, for example AMM01-0001.
If you see Possible duplicate, a patient with the same national ID or file number is already in the registry (at any center). Check before continuing. A patient can be enrolled only once.
6

Fill the patient record

The record is split into tabs on the left: Enrollment, Identifiers, Demographics, Presentation, Etiology, Investigations, KCCQ-12, Medications, Devices and Index admission (inpatients only). Fill them in any order, over several days if needed.

  • A green tick on a tab means its required fields are done. A red number shows how many required fields are still empty.
  • Required fields have a red star. If a test was not done or the answer is unknown, press Not done or Unknown next to the field name.
  • Age, BMI, eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021), HF phenotype and QRS category are calculated automatically.
  • For lab values, choose the unit your lab uses (for example creatinine in mg/dL or µmol/L). The system converts it.
  • Impossible values are blocked (for example LVEF 95%). Unusual values show a yellow warning so you can double-check.
Your work is never lost. Every change is kept on your device straight away and saved to the server automatically a few seconds after you stop typing. The bar at the bottom says All changes saved when it is done. You can also press Save at any time.
7

Medications

In the Medications tab, record every heart failure medicine at this time point.

  1. For each class (ARNI/ACEi/ARB, beta-blocker, MRA, SGLT2 inhibitor) use + Add drug and choose the drug.
  2. Enter the dose each time in mg and how often. The bar shows the % of the guideline target dose automatically.
  3. If a class is not prescribed, or the dose is below target, choose the reason (hypotension, hyperkalemia, cost, titration in progress…).
  4. Tick All heart failure medicines at this time point are recorded, even if the patient takes none.

Between visits, use Log a medication change (start, increase, decrease, stop, switch). The next visit's medication list is pre-filled from this log, so you enter things only once.

8

Mark the record complete

When the baseline data are entered, press Mark complete at the top. If something is missing, a list appears. Click any item to jump to it.

After a record is complete, every change asks for a reason. This is saved in the change history (audit trail).

9

Follow-up visits

Jo-CHF calculates each visit window from the enrollment date:

30 days day 23–37 · phone or clinic
6 months day 150–210 · clinic preferred
12 months day 335–395 · clinic preferred
  1. Open Follow-ups in the menu, or the patient's Follow-up tab.
  2. Press Enter visit. Fill date, method and vital status. For alive patients, check the medicines (already pre-filled), adherence, side effects, labs if available, and KCCQ-12 at 6 and 12 months.
  3. Could not reach the patient? Press Log call for every attempt (date, time, number, outcome).
  4. After 3 unsuccessful calls on different days, Mark lost appears. Then check vital status in the hospital records. You can still reach the patient at the next window.
10

Hospital admissions and urgent visits

In the Events tab, press Add event for each hospital admission, and for each urgent or emergency visit for worsening HF treated with IV diuretics without admission.

The system decides automatically if an admission is an HF hospitalization: unplanned, 24 hours or longer, HF as the main reason, and IV diuretics, vasoactive therapy or mechanical support.

11

Death or withdrawal of consent

If the patient died, open Death & withdrawal and press Record death (date, place, source and cause). This also opens automatically if you choose "Dead" at a follow-up or "In-hospital death". Future visits are cancelled automatically.

If the patient withdraws consent, record the date and whether data already collected can be kept.

12

Answer data queries

The coordinating team may ask about a value (for example "Potassium 6.4 on MRA, please confirm"). You see a red number next to Data queries and a yellow note on the field itself.

  1. Open the query.
  2. Check the source document and correct the value in the record if needed.
  3. Press Reply and say what you did.

Only administrators can delete records. If a record was created by mistake, use the trash button in the patient header to request deletion with a reason.

13

Patient journey and care opportunities

The Patient journey tab shows the whole year on one line: visits, admissions, the four foundational drug classes (darker bar = closer to target dose) and the KCCQ score.

Guideline care opportunities are automatic reminders compared with ESC guidance, for example "SGLT2i not prescribed" or "iron deficiency without IV iron". They support your review; you decide.

14

Analytics

Analytics in the menu shows live results for your center: patient profile, drug uptake and titration over time, survival curves, hospitalizations, KCCQ, labs and devices. Use the filters at the top (phenotype, setting, sex). Every chart has a button to see the numbers as a table and to save it as an image.

Questions

The internet stopped while I was typing. Is my data lost?

No. Changes are kept on your device. When the connection is back, open the patient again and press Save. If you closed the page, Jo-CHF offers to restore your unsaved changes.

Can I see patients from another center?

No. You can only open records of your own center. For other centers you see the summary numbers on the dashboard.

Can administrators see my patients' names?

No. Names, phone numbers, file numbers and national IDs are encrypted and visible only to your center. Administrators see only the Study ID. Exports never include identifiers.

I made a mistake in a completed record.

Correct it and give a short reason when asked. The old and new values are kept in the change history.

Can I use my phone?

Yes. Jo-CHF works in the browser of any phone or tablet. You can also add it to your home screen from the browser menu.

Who do I contact for help?

Contact the study administrators (Dr. Imad, Dr. Ayman, Sofian Zreigh).

Try the demo first

The demo is a full copy of Jo-CHF with more than 500 invented patients across 12 centers. It runs only in your browser: nothing is sent anywhere, and you can reset it any time. Use it for training before entering real patients.

Open Jo-CHF

For administrators

Administrators (the principal investigators and the coordinator) see all centers with de-identified data, approve accounts, check data quality and export the dataset.

A1

Approve accounts

Open Users. New requests are at the top. Press Approve, confirm the role and the center, and press Approve and activate. Use Deactivate when someone leaves a center: their data stay. The refresh button gives a temporary password if someone forgets theirs.

A2

Centers, targets and study settings

In Centers & targets, add each center with its code (the start of every Study ID) and its recruitment target. In Study settings, choose whether follow-up windows for inpatients start at enrollment or discharge, check the target doses against the final ESC 2026 tables, and paste the licensed Arabic KCCQ-12 wording once the license is confirmed.

A3

Data quality: queries, locking and adjudication

Open any patient and use Data queries → Raise a query on a field. The site sees it, answers and corrects. Close the query when it is resolved. After cleaning, press Lock in the patient header so no more changes can be made. In Death & withdrawal, press Adjudicate to confirm or change the cause of death; both versions are kept.

A4

Export and audit

Export data creates Excel, CSV, SPSS or R files with a data dictionary. Exports are always de-identified, and dates can be exported as days from enrollment. The Audit log shows every sign-in, change and deletion with old and new values. It cannot be edited by anyone.
